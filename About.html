About Our School

KATLEHONG SECONDARY SCHOOL

Transforming Lives Through Quality Education

Katlehong Secondary School has been a cornerstone of educational excellence in our community for nearly four decades. Founded in 1985, we have grown from a small township school to a leading institution that prepares students for success in higher education and beyond.

Our dedicated staff of experienced educators work tirelessly to create a nurturing environment where every learner can reach their full potential. We combine rigorous academics with character development, sports, and cultural activities to develop well-rounded individuals.

Located in the heart of Katlehong, South Africa, we proudly serve over 1,200 learners from Grade 8 through Grade 12, with a pass rate that consistently exceeds provincial averages.
